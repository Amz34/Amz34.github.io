    7.1 The 5 Structured Advisory Stages

    Stage 1: Discover (Macro Scoping & Exposure Mapping)

    • Objective: Map the client's project portfolio against the unawarded $1.72tn pipeline.
    • Deliverable: Identification of top-3 ranked risk gates across active packages and supply chain exposure.
    • FDE Metric: Cycle time baseline and exposure to unhedged fixed-price packages.

    Stage 2: Diagnose (Supply Chain, Blowback & Regulatory Audit)

    • Objective: Stress-test supply chains against material price spikes and regulatory compliance thresholds.
    • Audit Checklist:
      • Long-lead MEP items (chillers, switchgear, generators) flagged against European/Asian manufacturing lead times.
      • Iktva local content percentage audited against 2030 statutory targets.
      • Nitaqat engineering workforce Saudization ratios verified.
    • FDE Metric: Vulnerability scorecard (0–100) and localized supply chain redundancy plan.

    [!WARNING] Regulatory Tripwire Alert Failing to maintain Platinum Nitaqat status immediately locks the company's Qiwa portal, halting visa issuances and blocking municipal building permits.

    Stage 3: Design (Forward Deployed AI & Schedule Governance)

    • Objective: Establish the digital data spine between Oracle Primavera P6, Autodesk Revit/BIM, and site telemetry.
    • Deliverable: Automated critical path schedule reconciliation model that flags milestone variance 30 days early.
    • FDE Metric: Reduction in scheduled clash rework and contractual dispute exposure.

    Stage 4: Deliver (Site Execution & Cash Flow Compression)

    • Objective: Accelerate on-site progress verification and interim payment certificate (IPC) processing.
    • Deliverable: Deployment of multimodal tablet telemetry for field engineers, automated quantity surveying via drone point-clouds, and digital milestone sign-offs.
    • FDE Metric: Compression of the cash settlement cycle from 210 days to 68 days.

    [!TIP] Commercial Authority on Cash Flow Implementing automated drone volumetric surveys for earthworks and excavation cuts sign-off cycles from 45 days to under 48 hours, eliminating working capital bottlenecks.

    Stage 5: Scale (Long-Term Operational IFM & Asset Handover)

    • Objective: Ensure seamless, data-rich transition from construction completion to operational facilities management.
    • Deliverable: Digital Twin asset repository populated with verified O&M manuals, serial numbers, warranty terms, and predictive maintenance algorithms.
    • FDE Metric: 62% reduction in initial-year unplanned equipment downtime and 18% energy savings.
