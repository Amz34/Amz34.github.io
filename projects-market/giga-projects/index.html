    Giga-Projects Portfolio

    Phasing, Capital Allocation & Execution Realignment

    The giga-projects portfolio under the Public Investment Fund (PIF) represents the global showcase of Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 transformation. Across the portfolio, developers have transitioned from simultaneous mega-rollouts to disciplined, phased module delivery.

    Figure 5.1: Active civil engineering groundworks, foundation piling, and crane mobilization across Saudi giga-development corridors.

    1. NEOM ($500 Billion+)

    The flagship development covering 26,500 square kilometers in northwest Saudi Arabia has reorganized its procurement around phased functional zones:

    • Sindalah: The luxury island destination in the Red Sea successfully reached initial handover and operations, establishing NEOM's first operational tourism footprint.
    • The Line: Construction has concentrated on early foundation packages and initial 2.4-kilometer modules rather than the entirety of the 170-kilometer corridor, focusing on modular prefabrication and high-speed rail tunneling.
    • Trojena: Prioritized for completion ahead of the 2029 Asian Winter Games, with major tunneling, dam construction, and mountain resort infrastructure advancing rapidly.
    • Oxagon: The floating industrial city and deep-water port serving as the supply chain marshaling hub for the entire northwest region and home to the NEOM Green Hydrogen Company.

    2. Diriyah Company ($63 Billion)

    Located on the outskirts of Riyadh, Diriyah is the historic birthplace of the Kingdom, transforming into a global cultural, heritage, and lifestyle capital:

    • At-Turaif UNESCO World Heritage Site: Integrated with pedestrianized historic quarters, museums, and high-end Najdi-style boutique hotels.
    • Diriyah Square & Opera House: Massive excavation and civil packages totaling over $10B awarded to Tier-1 joint ventures, including El-Seif and Almabani.
    • Phased Execution: Phased handover schedule targeting initial residential and retail delivery by 2026, with full cultural assets completed ahead of Expo 2030.

    3. New Murabba & The Mukaab ($50 Billion)

    Covering 19 square kilometers in northwest Riyadh, New Murabba is designed as the world's largest modern downtown:

    • The Mukaab: A 400-meter-tall, 400-meter-wide cubic super-structure featuring holographic immersive hospitality and commercial spaces.
    • Civil Groundworks: Earthworks and excavation packages encompassing millions of cubic meters are actively underway, tracking a non-negotiable delivery date ahead of Expo 2030.

    4. ROSHN ($40 Billion)

    PIF's national community developer delivering integrated, walkable residential communities across the Kingdom:

    • SEDRA & WAREFA (Riyadh): Thousands of villas and townhomes delivered with underground utilities, solar street lighting, and community retail.
    • MARAFY & ALAROUS (Jeddah): Introducing an 11-kilometer artificial canal that connects coastal residential zones, reshaping Jeddah's urban waterfront.

    5. Red Sea Global ($30 Billion)

    The world's leading regenerative luxury tourism destination across 90 offshore islands and inland volcanic landscapes:

    • The Red Sea Destination: Multiple luxury island resorts (St. Regis, Ritz-Carlton Reserve, Six Senses) operational and welcoming international guests.
    • Amaala (Triple Bay): Focused on sports, wellness, and medical tourism, with 29 hotels and 3,900 keys currently under advanced construction.
    • 100% Renewable Off-Grid: Powered exclusively by solar panels, wind farms, and the world's largest off-grid battery energy storage system (BESS).

    6. Qiddiya ($25 Billion)

    The capital of entertainment, sports, and culture situated 45 kilometers southwest of Riyadh:

    • Six Flags Qiddiya & Aquarabia: Mega-theme park featuring world-record roller coasters nearing physical testing and commissioning.
    • Speed Park Track & Gaming District: Dedicated Formula 1 racing circuits and esports arenas engineered to host major international championships.
